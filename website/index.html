WHEN YOU’RE READY TO HIT RECORD

The whole studio.
Right on your Mac.

The browser gets you started. The native app keeps you in the moment.

01 / VOICE

It follows you.

Local Whisper follows your script or adapts to your cadence. Pause, skip a word, take a breath. Experimental hands-free commands keep you in front of the camera.

On-device processing · No cloud AI
02 / OUTPUT

Every seat in the studio.

Keep producer controls on your Mac. Send the script to a talent monitor, or read in Webcam Layout close to your camera. Mirror the text for prompter glass.

External displays · Webcam Layout
03 / COMPANION

Your iPad, on cue.

Turn an iPad into your reading display. Pair once on the same network. Scrolling, bookmarks, and appearance stay in sync with the Mac.
